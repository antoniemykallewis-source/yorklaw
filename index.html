How a case gets built from the records

  1. Every shift leaves paper behind.

    Care logs, staffing sheets, medication records, state inspection reports. Facilities write it all down.

  2. Neglect hides in the gaps.

    A turn charted while the resident was already at the hospital. A call light left ringing for 41 minutes. A night shift covered by one aide.

  3. We line it up.

    We request and subpoena the records, build the timeline, and find where the facility's story stops matching.

  4. Then the operator answers for it.

    At the mediation table, or in front of a jury.

Cases we take on

Harm in nursing homes, assisted living and memory care, and the families left to make sense of it.

  • Nursing home and assisted living neglect Understaffing, missed care and preventable decline.
  • Elder abuse Physical, emotional or sexual abuse of an older adult by staff or caregivers.
  • Wrongful death When neglect or abuse in a care setting costs a life.
  • Bedsores and pressure injuries Injuries that basic turning and skin care are meant to prevent.
  • Physical and chemical restraints Restraints or sedating drugs used for discipline or convenience instead of care.
  • Financial abuse and fraud Undue influence and exploitation that take advantage of residents.
